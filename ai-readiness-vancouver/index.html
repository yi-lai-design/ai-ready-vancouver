What "AI readiness" means

AI readiness is whether a website can be found, correctly understood, and acted on by AI assistants — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and the AI features now built into Google itself. It's a different, newer question than the one SEO answers.

SEO asks: can a search engine rank this page? AI readiness asks something further downstream: once an AI system has your page in front of it, can it actually tell what your business does, where you are, what you charge, and how someone books you — well enough to recommend you by name?

Those two things are more independent than most business owners expect. We ran a real audit on 50 well-reviewed Vancouver businesses across 10 industries, and every single one failed at least one AI readiness check — including several with excellent SEO. A site can rank first on Google and still be functionally invisible to an AI assistant reading it.

Why this matters for a Vancouver business specifically

Vancouver's economy runs on independent businesses — restaurants, contractors, clinics, salons, shops — competing hard for the same local customers. A five-star reputation built over years used to be enough to win that competition on Google. Increasingly, it isn't, because a growing share of that same search behaviour is happening inside an AI assistant instead of a search results page.

When someone asks an AI assistant "who's a good plumber near me" or "what's the best-reviewed dentist in East Van," the assistant can only recommend businesses whose websites it can actually parse. A five-star rating that never reaches the tools people now ask first might as well not exist to that customer.

This project exists because that gap is measurable, not theoretical: we checked 50 real, well-reviewed Vancouver businesses across 10 industries against the checks above, and 50 out of 50 failed at least one of them. It varies sharply by trade — some industries have machine-findable contact info nearly everywhere, others almost nowhere.

Questionsyou might be asking

Is AI readiness only relevant to Vancouver businesses?
No — the underlying checks apply to any website. AI Ready Vancouver started local because that's where we could actually go verify real businesses ourselves, not because the problem is Vancouver-specific.
How is AI readiness different from local SEO?
Local SEO gets your business found on Google Maps and local search results. AI readiness is about whether an AI assistant that already found your page can actually read and correctly understand it well enough to recommend you. A business can rank well locally and still be invisible to AI.
What's the AI Ready Score?
It's how many of the 4 real checks a site passes, counted like a home inspection rather than as a percentage: a machine-readable summary, machine-findable contact info, a stable page layout, and an AI-actionable next step beyond a phone number.
