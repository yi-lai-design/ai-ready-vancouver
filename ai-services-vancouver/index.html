What people mean by "AI services" in Vancouver

"AI services" is a loose umbrella covering everything from a one-afternoon strategy session to a six-figure custom build. Vancouver has a dense cluster of AI consultancies, automation agencies, and independent developers offering it, and the labels they use rarely line up.

This page is a neutral map of that landscape: what the common service types actually do, how engagements are usually priced, and the questions worth asking before you sign anything.

We don't sell any of it. AI Ready Vancouver is a free, independent project run by two local tech professionals. We run one free check — whether AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity can find and understand your business — and send back a plain-English report. That's the whole offering. Everything below is here because people kept asking us how to think about hiring, and there wasn't a straight answer that wasn't also a sales pitch.

The main typesof AI services

Most Vancouver providers offer some mix of these. The names vary; the underlying work doesn't much.

explore

Strategy & consulting

Someone maps where AI realistically fits your business and in what order. Good for teams that don't know where to start. Worth checking: that the deliverable is a plan you can act on, not just a slide deck.

bolt

Workflow & process automation

Connecting your tools so repetitive work runs itself, often with a language model handling one step. For most small businesses this is the fastest, most measurable return.

forum

Chatbots & support AI

An assistant on your site or phone line that answers common questions and books jobs. Only as good as the content it's grounded in — a bot can't state hours your website never clearly states.

build

Custom AI tools & agents

Bespoke software built around your own data: a quoting assistant, a document processor, an internal search tool. The highest cost and the highest ceiling.

school

Training & workshops

Teaching your team to use tools that already exist. Low cost, high leverage, and it doesn't require anyone to build or maintain custom software.

database

Data & integration work

Getting your data clean, connected, and accessible enough that any of the above can actually work. Unglamorous, and usually the real prerequisite.

How AI services are priced

There's no standard rate for AI work in Vancouver — scope varies too much. What's more useful is knowing which pricing model you're being quoted, and what each one is really charging for.

A framework for reading quotes, not a price list. Ask every provider to be explicit about which model they're using and what falls outside it.
ModelYou'll see it forWhat you're paying forWorth asking
Fixed-fee project Automations, custom tools, one-off assessments A defined deliverable, handed over once "What happens when it breaks in three months?"
Monthly retainer Ongoing automation, managed AI, chatbot upkeep Maintenance, changes, and someone to call "What's the minimum term, and what's included each month?"
Per-seat / per-usage Chatbots, SaaS-style tools Volume — messages, users, or API calls "How does the cost scale if this works really well?"
Hourly / time & materials Consulting, discovery, exploratory work The provider's time, not a guaranteed outcome "What's the estimate, and what would push it higher?"

Questions to askany AI services provider

None of these are gotchas. A good provider will have clear answers ready.

history_edu

"Can you show me a comparable project?"

A reference in your industry or roughly your size. AI demos look impressive; ask what happened six months after go-live.

key

"What do I own at the end?"

Code, prompts, training data, the accounts and API keys. Get the answer in writing before work starts, not after.

warning

"What breaks this?"

A model version change, an API price increase, a key person leaving. You want to hear they've thought about it.

query_stats

"How will we know it worked?"

One number, agreed before the work begins — hours saved, response time, conversion. "It'll feel faster" is not a metric.

Is AI Ready Vancouver an AI services company?
No. AI Ready Vancouver is a free, independent project run by two Vancouver tech professionals. What kinds of AI services can a Vancouver small business actually use?
The common ones are strategy and consulting, workflow automation, customer-facing chatbots, custom AI tools built around your data, team training on existing tools, and the data cleanup and integration work that makes the rest possible. For most small businesses, workflow automation and training deliver the fastest, most measurable return.
How are AI services priced in Vancouver?
Four models cover most engagements: a fixed fee for a defined project, a monthly retainer for ongoing work and support, per-seat or per-usage pricing for chatbots and SaaS-style tools, and hourly time-and-materials for exploratory or consulting work. There's no standard rate — scope varies enormously — so compare the pricing model and what it actually covers, not just the number.
Do I need an AI services provider, or can I start on my own?
Many businesses start with free or low-cost tools and a training session before hiring anyone.
