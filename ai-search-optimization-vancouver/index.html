What "AI search optimization" actually covers

AI search optimization is the practice of making a website findable, correctly interpretable, and citable by AI assistants and the AI features now built into search engines — not just ranked by a classic search algorithm.

It sits downstream of traditional SEO. SEO gets a page crawled and ranked. AI search optimization is about what happens after that: whether an AI system that has found your page can actually extract what your business does, where it's located, and how someone can act on that — accurately enough to recommend you by name.

A business can do everything right by classic SEO standards — fast page, clean structure, strong backlinks — and still be functionally invisible to an AI assistant reading the same page, if that page never states its services in a way a machine can parse.

Questionsyou might be asking

Is AI search optimization the same for ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity?
The foundation is the same — a machine-readable, stable, well-structured page helps with all of them. But each surface sources answers differently: Perplexity leans heavily on live citations, Gemini often grounds in Google Search results, and ChatGPT's search mode uses its own crawler and indexing. Getting the foundation right helps across all of them; there's no single trick that works identically everywhere.
Do I need separate strategies for each AI engine?
Not separate strategies, but it helps to understand where each one gets its information, since that affects what's worth prioritizing. The 4 core checks (machine-readable summary, machine-findable contact info, stable layout, AI-actionable next step) are a foundation that helps regardless of which AI system a customer happens to ask.
Does AI search optimization replace SEO?
No, it sits alongside it. SEO gets your page ranked and crawled in the first place. AI search optimization is about what happens once an AI system has your page in front of it — whether it can actually understand and use what it finds.
